little big heart

Small box, big meaning

little big heart is a small box with a door. Your loved ones send messages and photos to it from their phones. When a message arrives, a red heart lights up on the door, and when you open the door the message is waiting on the screen.

Retail price after the campaign, including VAT and delivery to an Omniva parcel locker in Estonia. During the campaign the box is only available through crowdfunding on Indiegogo. Boxes ship by 9 February 2027.

€65 on Indiegogo

Indiegogo campaign from 31 October to 29 December 2026: the first 100 boxes are €65.

There are so many messages on a phone that the most important one gets lost among the rest.

little big heart conveys special moments in the right way

It sits on a bedside table or a shelf and waits. When someone close to you sends you a message, the red heart on the door of the box lights up and starts to breathe gently. You open the door and the message or photo is on the screen. The sender can see that you have read it, and when you tap the message, a heart goes back to them.

Three steps

How it works

  1. 1

    Plug the box in and connect it to your home Wi-Fi

    The QR code on the screen links the box to your phone.

  2. 2

    Invite the senders

    Your partner, children, grandparents, friends. A sender only needs a phone.

  3. 3

    The sender writes a message or picks a photo

    The heart lights up and you open the door.

Small box, many skills

What the box can do

Messages and photos

From a phone to a 3.5-inch touchscreen.

A breathing heart

The heart breathes until the message has been read; the sender is told when the door was opened.

A heart back

Tap a message and a heart goes back to the sender.

Scheduled messages

Write it today and it reaches the box on the morning of the birthday.

Paired boxes

The heart of one box makes the heart of the other box breathe.

Night mode

At night the heart and the screen dim.

Memories

The box reminds you of the message that arrived exactly a year ago.

Gift card

Write the first message before you give the box as a gift; when the recipient switches it on, your message is already waiting.

Who it is for

  • For couples

    Who spend the day in different places.

  • For people in long-distance relationships

  • For grandparents

    Whose grandchildren send them photos without anyone having to wrestle with a smartphone.

  • For friends

    Who want to say “thinking of you” without yet another chat.

Privacy

The box has no camera or microphone

The box does not send any data anywhere about when it is used.

Messages travel through a server in the European Union, and only the people the box owner has invited can see them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the sender need a box too?

No. A sender only needs a phone.

How many people can send to one box?

The box owner invites the senders and can remove them at any time.

What does the box need?

A power socket and a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. The USB-C power adapter and cable are included.

Does the box run on a battery?

No, little big heart is always plugged in.

Can I give the box as a gift?

Yes, the box comes with a gift card that lets you write the first message before you give it.

What if delivery is delayed?

We will let you know right away and give you a new date; if that does not suit you, you get your money back.

Can you deliver outside Estonia?

Yes, through the Indiegogo campaign we deliver all over Europe; shipping is extra and depends on the country. If you would like delivery outside Europe, write to us at info@look360.eu and we will find a solution.

Does the box have a warranty?

Yes, the statutory right to submit a claim for at least two years, or longer if the law of your country of residence provides for it.
