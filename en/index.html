The message that matters most stays in sight

Our phones hold so many messages that the most important one gets lost among the rest. little big heart is for one person. It sits on a bedside table or a shelf and waits.

When someone close to you sends a message, the red heart on the door lights up and starts to breathe gently. You open the door and the message or photo is on the screen. The sender sees that you have read it, and when you tap the message, a heart goes back to them.

How it works

  1. Plug the box in and connect it to your home Wi-Fi. A QR code on the screen links the box to your phone.
  2. Invite the senders: your partner, children, grandparents, friends. A sender only needs a phone.
  3. The sender writes a message or picks a photo. The heart lights up and you open the door.

What the box can do

Messages and photos

From a phone to a 3.5-inch touchscreen.

Read receipts

The heart breathes until the message is read. The sender is told when the door was opened.

A heart back

Tap the message and a heart goes back to the sender.

Scheduled messages

Write it today and it arrives in the box on the morning of the birthday.

Paired boxes

The heart of one box makes the heart of the other box breathe.

Night mode

At night the heart and the screen dim.

Memories

The box reminds you of the message that arrived exactly a year ago.

Gift card

Write the first message before you give the box. When the recipient switches it on, your message is already waiting.

Who it is for

Couples who spend the day in different places. People in long-distance relationships. Grandparents whose grandchildren send them photos without anyone having to wrestle with a smartphone. Friends who want to say "thinking of you" without starting yet another chat.

Privacy

The box has no camera and no microphone. It does not send data anywhere about when it is used. Messages travel through a server in the European Union and only the people the box owner has invited can see them.

Frequently asked questions

Does the sender need a box too?

No. A sender only needs a phone. The app opens in the browser and can be added to the home screen.

How many people can send to one box?

The box owner invites the senders and can remove them at any time.

What does the box need?

A power socket and a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. The USB-C power adapter and cable are included.

Does the box run on a battery?

No. The box is meant to stay in one place and is always plugged in.

Can I give the box as a gift?

Yes. The box comes with a gift card that lets you write the first message before you give it.

What happens if the campaign does not reach its goal?

Under Hooandja's all-or-nothing principle, you get your money back.

What if delivery is delayed?

We will let you know as soon as a delay becomes clear and give you a new date. If the new date does not suit you, you get your money back.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, the two-year statutory right to submit a claim.

Who makes it?

We are Marko and Marcus from Tartu, Estonia. We make the body and the door ourselves in our workshop in Tartu. The prototype works: the box, screen, heart and phone app are in everyday use on our test boxes.
