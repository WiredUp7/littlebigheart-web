1. The service

little big heart is a service by Watchdog OÜ (registry code 17537572, Sepa tn 4-2, 71003 Viljandi, Estonia, info@look360.eu) that lets you send messages and photos from your phone to a little big heart box and manage your boxes. The service consists of the app (on the web and on Google Play), the server and the box. These terms apply to using the app and the service. When you buy a box, the terms of sale also apply, and personal data is processed under the privacy policy.

The box owner decides who can send messages to their box and can remove senders at any time. Senders are responsible for the messages, photos and videos they send.
